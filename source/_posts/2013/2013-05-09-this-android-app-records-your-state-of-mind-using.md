This Android app records your state of mind using a [NeuroSky MindWave](http://neurosky.com/Products/MindWave.aspx) headset.

Even if you aren’t interested in having your mind read, this app shows how to authenticate with Zenobase using OAuth2, and post data on behalf of a user through the [API](https://zenobase.com/#/api/).

[Download](https://docs.google.com/uc?export=download&id=0B6V71rdiuHLJOGM3OXhLQUxaLVk) the app or [browse the source code](https://github.com/zenobase/zenobase-mindlog).
