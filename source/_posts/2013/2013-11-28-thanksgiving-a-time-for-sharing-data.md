Have you ever wanted to share self-tracking data, but then dropped the idea because letting strangers know your current whereabouts–or certain habits–didn’t seem like a good idea?

Sharing self-tracking data need not be an all-or-nothing proposition: Watch this demo to see how you can share a filtered view of your data with [Zenobase](https://zenobase.com/). (The demo uses data imported from Foursquare, but applies to any kind of data).

<iframe width="480" height="360" src="//www.youtube.com/embed/SEknu8xAbWc" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen=""></iframe>

Incidentally, data that is shared this way is also accessible through the [Zenobase API](https://zenobase.com/#/api/)–giving you in effect a _personal API_ with zero coding.
