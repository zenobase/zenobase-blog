Zenobase Cardio is a simple Android (4.3+) app for performing resting heart rate tests using a Bluetooth LE heart rate monitor such as the [Polar H7](http://www.polar.com/en/products/accessories/H7_heart_rate_sensor).

This app can be used as a template for apps that record data to Zenobase through the [API](https://zenobase.com/#/api/); in particular, this app demonstrates how to use OAuth2 to obtain an authorization and send data to Zenobase on behalf of a user.

![](tumblr_inline_myfbiiZF4L1rqupev.png)

Check out the [source](https://github.com/zenobase/zenobase-cardio) or [download](https://github.com/zenobase/zenobase-cardio/releases/download/r1/zenobase-cardio.apk) the app.
