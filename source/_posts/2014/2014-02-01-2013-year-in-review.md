**Highlights from last year:**

*   Added support for more services, including BodyMedia, Netatmo and RunKeeper.
*   Published [an official API](https://zenobase.com/#/api/), along with [a sample app](https://github.com/zenobase/zenobase-cardio).
*   Recorded [some screencasts](https://www.youtube.com/user/zenobase), showing e.g. how to do correlations and determine effect sizes.
*   [Presented](https://vimeo.com/79674487) at the Quantified Self conferences in Amsterdam and San Francisco, and participated in many Seattle-area meetups.
*   Created a [privacy policy](https://zenobase.com/#/legal/) that prevents us from selling your data–or changing the terms behind your back.
*   Introduced [paid plans](https://zenobase.com/#/pricing/) (necessary, now that we can’t sell your data :-)

**What’s next?**

*   Support for more services, e.g. Moves and Jawbone. Which would you like to see?
*   Do more screencasts and tutorials. Which features are least clear to you?
*   Help developers integrate their apps with Zenobase.
