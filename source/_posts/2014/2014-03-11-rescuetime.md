Zenobase can now get hourly productivity scores from [RescueTime](https://www.rescuetime.com/). I haven’t collected enough data with RescueTime yet to do a proper demo, but here are some questions that might be worth asking once I do:

*   Does my productivity differ significantly between summer and winter?
*   Is there a significant correlation between my sleep and my productivity?
*   Does heavy exercise reduce my productivity the next day?
