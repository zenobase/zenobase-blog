You can now analyze your [Strava](http://www.strava.com/) data on [zenobase.com](http://zenobase.com). What affects your activities more, sleep or weather?

To get started, chose the _Activities (Strava)_ template when creating a new bucket, or add a _strava-activities_ task to an existing bucket. Then use the _forecast_ task to add weather data, or create views to combine with data from other buckets.
