[Sleep as Android](https://sites.google.com/site/sleepasandroid/home) is a popular app for logging sleep. Data stored on the app’s [SleepCloud](https://sites.google.com/site/sleepasandroid/sleepcloud) service can now be analyzed on [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/).

<iframe width="480" height="360" src="//www.youtube.com/embed/_R_uof2QUXs" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen=""></iframe>

The data includes begin and duration, percent of time spent in deep sleep, rating (if any), and the number of “sleep cycles”.

The simplest way to get started is to create a new bucket, choosing the _Sleep (SleepCloud)_ template.

~**Caveat:** SleepCloud trusts any app that has authenticated you with Google. This means that any service listed on Google’s [Account Permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions) page has access to your sleep data.~ (fixed as of April 27)
