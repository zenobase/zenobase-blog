You can now analyze data from the [MapMyFitness](http://mapmyfitness.com/) family of apps (incl MapMyRun) on [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/).

To get started, choose the _Activities (MapMyFitness)_ template when creating a new bucket, or add a _mapmyfitness-activities_ task to an existing bucket. Then use the forecast task to add weather data, or create views to combine with data from other buckets…
