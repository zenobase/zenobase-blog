The [Automatic](https://www.automatic.com/) is a small device that connects to your car and records data for each trip (incl. time, distance and mpg).

You can now connect this service to [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/); to get started, choose the _Trips (Automatic)_ template when creating a new bucket, or add a _automatic-trips_ task to an existing bucket.

If you don’t have an Automatic yet (and you’re fast enough), you can [get one here for $20 off](https://www.automatic.com/order/?ref=ERIC685).
