With the addition of support for importing sleep and step data from [Jawbone UP](https://jawbone.com/up/), we now support all major fitness trackers (that have an open API).

![](tumblr_inline_ncu4dwzC4l1rqupev.png)

You can of course still [suggest additional data sources here](http://zenobase.uservoice.com/), and don’t forget that with a bit of coding, you can write your own integrations as well!
