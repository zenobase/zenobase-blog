You can now analyze data from [Misfit](http://misfit.com/) devices (including the Shine, the Flash and the Beddit) on [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/). To get started, simply choose the corresponding _activities_, _steps_ or _sleep_ template when creating a new bucket.
