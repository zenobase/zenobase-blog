**Highlights from 2014:**

*   Integrated Automatic, iHealth, Jawbone, Google Fit, Last.fm, MapMyFitness, Misfit, Moves, Reporter, RescueTime, Sleep as Android, SleepCycle, Strava, Trackthisforme, and Withings.
    
*   Introduced a few new data types, including currency, pace, and volume per distance (as mpg or kpl), along with a few other minor changes to [our API](https://zenobase.com/#/api/).
    
*   [Posted sample code](http://blog.zenobase.com/tagged/api) for importing data from custom spreadsheets, as well as from HealthKit.
    
*   Gave some talks at Quantified Self meetups, and [recorded a few screencasts](https://www.youtube.com/user/zenobase) that show how to make best use of Zenobase.
    

**What’s next?**

*   Support more services and figure out more useful visualizations and statistics. Let us know what you’d like to see.
    
*   The cost of the paid plan will increase to $7/month in January. Existing paid customers remain at $5/month.
    
*   We’ll set aside an hour each day to help people free of charge with their self-tracking projects. (You can also [hire us](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ejain) for consulting or contract work on commercial projects).
    

To see what we are up to, follow us on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/zenobase), [Google+](https://plus.google.com/+Zenobase) or [Facebook](https://facebook.com/zenobase).
