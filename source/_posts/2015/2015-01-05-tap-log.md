[Tap Log](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.waterbear.taglog) is another, very convenient Android app for logging data by hand. The app exports CSV files, which you can now directly import into any bucket on [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/).

![](nhp4j2s41W1rqupev.png) Most fields, including categories, timestamps, ratings, notes and locations are automatically mapped to the corresponding fields; you’ll only have to specify what field (and unit) to use for Tap Log’s _number_ field.
