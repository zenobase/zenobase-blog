[Nomie](https://nomie.io/) is the latest app from which you can import data into [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/). Similar to [Trackthisforme](http://blog.zenobase.com/post/107137542112) and [Tap Log](http://blog.zenobase.com/post/107202778882), Nomie tries to make logging data by hand as painless as possible. You can export your data to a spreadsheet, which you can then import into any bucket on Zenobase.

![](ni6ysjLwvR1rqupev.png) The mapping from Nomie’s data model is straightforward: _trackers_ are tags, _positivity_ numbers are ratings, and _lat_ and _long_ values are locations. _Notes_ are events with a note, tagged with “Note”.

![](ni6ysqLDoF1rqupev.png)

The latest version of Nomie is still in beta, but available for both Android and iOS.
