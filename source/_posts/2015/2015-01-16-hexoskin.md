The [Hexoskin](http://www.hexoskin.com/) is an athletic shirt with an integrated heart rate monitor and accelerometer. This makes it a good option for people who find chest straps too cumbersome to use for extended periods of time.

Hexoskin’s own dashboard shows the recorded data in full detail; for analyzing long-term trends, or for correlating the data with data from other sources, there is now the option to sync your data from Hexoskin to [Zenobase](http://zenobase.com).

![](tumblr_inline_ni9cjnAbtQ1rqupev.png)

Currently, there are two separate tasks that can be set up in Zenobase to sync data from Hexoskin:

*   **Activity summary data** includes a begin and end time, duration, average heart rate, step count, distance (in km or mi), and a link back to the detailed data on the Hexoskin website.
    
*   **Sleep summary data** is similar to activity summary data, but includes a sleep efficiency (as a percentage) instead of the step count and distance.
    

The easiest way to get started is to create a new _bucket_ in Zenobase, and chose the corresponding template for your Hexoskin data.

There is a lot more data in Hexoskin that could be useful in Zenobase, so [let us know](mailto:support@zenobase.com) what you’d like to do!
