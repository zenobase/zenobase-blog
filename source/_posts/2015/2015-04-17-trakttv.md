[Trakt.tv](https://trakt.tv/) is a great service for keeping track of what TV shows and movies you have watched (much like Last.fm for music, or Goodreads for books). You can “check in“ when you watch a movie, or even set up “scrobbling” to automatically log movies watched through certain media players.  

This data can now be synced into Zenobase: The _trakt_ task retrieves not only the title and date and time, but also the duration and genre tags for each TV show or movie you have seen.  

Once in Zenobase, this data can be extended, e.g. by geotagging it with location data from the Moves app, or correlated with data from other sources, e.g. sleep data from Fitbit. [Get in touch](mailto:hello@zenobase.com), and we’ll be glad to help you with your project!
