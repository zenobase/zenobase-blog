Curious how your mood is affected by factors like sleep or exercise, and how your mood affects other aspects of your life? You can store and analyze mood data in Zenobase, and now there is also support for retrieving mood data from the [Jawbone UP](https://jawbone.com/up) app, and from [MoodPanda](http://moodpanda.com/).

![Jawbone UP](nnjeefFh8f1rqupev_540.png)![MoodPanda](nnjeeoJe8t1rqupev_540.png)

If there are other mood tracking apps that you’d like us to support, [let us know](mailto:hello@zenobase.com)!
