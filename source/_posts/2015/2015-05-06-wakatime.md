In addition to [RescueTime](http://blog.zenobase.com/post/79247445112), we now support [WakaTime](https://wakatime.com/) as a source of time tracking data. Unlike RescueTime, WakaTime only tracks time spent in certain code editors, but can distinguish what project you are working on.

Once in Zenobase, you can analyze this data by day of week or hour of day, geotag it with location data from the Moves app, or correlate time spent on certain projects with sleep or calorie consumption etc.

If you are doing anything interesting with your time tracking data, [we’d love to hear about it](https://twitter.com/zenobase)!
