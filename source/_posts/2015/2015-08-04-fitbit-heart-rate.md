Owners of a Fitbit Charge HR or Surge can now import each day’s resting heart rate (or per-hour heart rate averages) into Zenobase for further analysis.

![](nsjrotT7Wl1rqupev_540.png)

**Note:** If you previously connected Fitbit to Zenobase, clear the Fitbit “credentials” first (as we need to ask for additional permissions to access the heart rate data).
