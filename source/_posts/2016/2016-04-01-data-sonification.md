Data visualization has its uses, but it’s easy to get overwhelmed when dealing with multiple data series. That’s where [data sonification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonification) comes in: Data sonification is a technique that has been used to help people sift through large amounts of data in applications ranging from deep space research to cancer research. This screencast shows how you can listen to your own data on [zenobase.com](https://zenobase.com/).

**Some limitations…** Only data points from timeline widgets in a dashboard are converted to sounds. The timelines are assumed to all have the same scale and range, i.e. no attempt is made to line up mismatched data points. Also, regardless of what statistic a timeline is set to show, the sounds are based on the average values (if available; else counts).
